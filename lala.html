🎧 How to Practice

  1. Use headphones

    Essential to clear your mind and focus deeply inward.

  2. Dedicate 15–20 minutes daily

    Find a quiet space, lie down or sit comfortably. You can just relax and listen, or repeat the words.

  3. One topic at a time

    Crucial rule: do not mix different tracks together. Your subconscious mind needs about 2 to 3 weeks to fully process and integrate one single affirmation. Give your mind time to rewire before moving on to the next topic.

🔮 A Note on Your Transformation

Subconscious reprogramming is like clearing a house that hasn’t been cleaned for years. When you start lifting old, heavy layers of inherited conditioning and past pain, the dust naturally rises.

You might suddenly experience unexplainable shifts: vivid dreams that feel like chaotic movies, unexpected waves of irritation, or sudden emotional static with those closest to you.

How to transform destructive energy into something beneficial? Through reflection. Through the ability to touch the core of the problem and the trauma. Through acceptance. Yes, I have anger; yes, I have pain; yes, I feel hurt. You ask yourself: "What am I feeling right now?" when a trigger goes off. "And why am I feeling this?" Not that someone else is to blame, but why am I reacting this way? What stands behind my reaction? When you look into your trauma and into your suppressed part, the movement already begins there. You take your behavioral pattern, belief, or projection and question EVERYTHING.

Trust the process, look at these storms with curiosity, and let your mind completely rebuild its foundation.
